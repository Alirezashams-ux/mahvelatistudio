    Comment on the proposed tabs
    Proposal · a new course by Ali (idea stage)

    Wrong on Purpose — let the AI be confidently wrong in front of the master

    Korea’s master craftsmen are retiring, and their know-how is mostly tacit — hard to put into words. Idea: an AI plays a naive apprentice and makes plausible-but-wrong statements; the master’s corrections, and the reasons for them, become verified “Know-how Cards”. This is a hypothesis to be piloted, not a proven method.

    Try it: correct the apprentice (fictional example — not real process advice)

    The 14-day outline (draft)
    Before this is offered to customers: pilot with 2–3 real factories, with the master’s written consent and a fair-benefit agreement; check that the method beats an ordinary interview; get Korean legal review for any legal content. Sources and evidence are in the separate research brief.
